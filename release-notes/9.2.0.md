Released on AntWeb's 24th birthday: Brian Fisher launched AntWeb on 17 October 2002. Thank you to every curator, photographer, museum and volunteer who has built it since. Thanks also to AntWeb's programmers Dave Thau (2002 - 2010) and Mark Johnson (2010 to 2025), and to our current volunteers Sathya (@Sathyavrv), Gladrin (@Gladrin22) and Antony (@antony-jr).

### For everyone
- AntWeb now works on phones: pages fit the screen, the menu folds behind a menu button, search is full width and pinch-zoom works.
- New "Keep me logged in" option on the login page (30 days), and a "Forgot password?" link that emails a one-time reset link.
- Security improvements across the site and servers.
- Small maps now use OpenStreetMap.
- Specimen pages show a specimen's **previous determinations** (earlier identifications, newest first) under "Date determined". California Academy of Sciences specimens get them from TaxonWorks every morning.
- Taxon pages link to the species' treatments at Plazi TreatmentBank, in place of an old copy whose links no longer worked. Dead links to HOL (Hymenoptera Online, offline for years) and AntShare are gone, and the Ant Course link goes to antcourse.org.
- The browser tab icon is easier to see in dark mode.
- Pages always load the current version of their scripts after an update.

### For curators
- **New page editor** for species, specimen, project and region pages: tables, links, images, sub- and superscript, and clean pasting from Word and Google Docs.
- **Edits are protected**: an empty box is never saved over existing text ("Clear this field" removes text on purpose); if someone else saved the same field meanwhile you see both versions; if a save fails (for example during the morning restart) your text is kept and offered again.
- **History**: a [History] link next to each field shows every earlier version, who saved it and what changed, and restores any version with one click.
- **TaxonWorks**: CAS specimens are updated from TaxonWorks every morning with the previous day's edits.
- **Image uploads of any size**: the Image Uploader now sends one image at a time with its own progress bar, so you can select 50 or 100 TIFFs at once (before, only 4 or 5 got through). One upload report lists them all, and its specimen links open the specimen pages.
- **New optional upload column `previousDeterminations`** for specimen text files: earlier identifications of the specimen, newest first, separated by " | ", for example `Nylanderia: US_CA01 by Ward on 2025-12-10 | Nylanderia: US_CA01 by Fisher on 2000-11-10`. Files without the column upload exactly as before.
- On specimen pages, "Upload File Line" shows the specimen's row as it was uploaded, and "Specimen's extra upload fields" shows the upload fields that have no other place on the page (both links had not worked since 2023). On taxon pages, the Worldants "Line Number" shows the taxon's row in the AntCat file.
- Curators can see the files in their own curator folder from the Curate page.
