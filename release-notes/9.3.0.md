AntWeb 9.3.0 improves the consistency, transparency and reliability of AntWeb's taxonomic data. Users can now tell curated regional knowledge from evidence based only on specimens, and every number on a place page can be traced to the list behind it.

For taxonomists, ecologists, conservation researchers and other users of biodiversity data, this means a species count for a country, bioregion, museum or project now says exactly which names it includes, and specimen records follow current AntCat taxonomy while the identification as uploaded is always kept.

This release also improves specimen uploads, curator tools, search and the API.

### Improvements for all users
- **Counts that match their lists.** The numbers of subfamilies, genera and species on a country, region, bioregion, museum or project page now equal the number of rows on the list each one opens. They are counted every day by the same rules, so anyone can check a number by opening its list.
- **AntWeb in numbers on the home page.** The home page shows how far AntWeb has come: the specimen records, the valid living ant species in AntCat (with the fossil species apart), how many of those species have specimens in AntWeb and how many have been imaged, and the images of ants and of labels. The statistics page gives the details. The numbers are counted every day and after each specimen upload.
- **Four views of every species and genus list.** Each view adds to the one before it (Fossil stands apart):

| View | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| Valid | Living species and genera that AntCat recognizes as valid, recorded from the place by specimens, by a curator, or in the literature |
| Curated (default) | Valid, plus the morphospecies a curator has recognized by adding them to a species list |
| All specimen names | Curated, plus every other morphospecies and the specimens identified only to genus |
| Fossil | Fossil species, and the genera and subfamilies that have fossil species in the place, extinct (marked †) and living |

- **Why Curated is the default.** It shows the place's curated knowledge: the valid species plus the morphospecies that curators have recognized. Uncurated morphospecies codes and specimens identified only to genus would otherwise inflate the counts; they remain one click away in All specimen names. A genus known in a place only from fossils is listed only in the Fossil view.
- **Fossil lists.** The Fossil genus and subfamily lists now show every genus and subfamily with fossil species in the place, living ones too, so their headers read, for example, "25 genera with fossil species". On the statistics page the Valid Fossil columns follow the same rule: "Subfamilies with fossil species" and "Genera with fossil species".
- **Where each name comes from.** Each row shows whether the name is on the place's curated list or is known there only from specimens collected in the place, so users can tell a formal regional record from specimen evidence.
- **Not curated.** Genus lists show, for each genus, how many specimens collected in the place carry a name that is not yet curated (an uncurated morphospecies, a specimen identified only to genus, or a name not in AntCat), with a link to those specimens. Under a species list, the Valid and Curated views also say how many specimens were identified only to genus, by genus, with a link.
- **Image counts of the ant itself.** On species rows the Images number counts every photograph of the ant in AntWeb, from any specimen; specimen label images are not counted. The linked images page shows the place's own specimens first. On genus rows the column says how many of the genus's species have an image.
- **Endemic and introduced** marks follow one documented rule and are given only to names on the place's curated list.
- **Search and navigation.** Sorting search results keeps the search. Lists of specimen codes can be pasted into a search. Map and Compare show results in pages of 200.
- **Licence.** AntWeb states its licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

### Improvements for curators
- **Specimens follow AntCat, and the uploaded identification is kept.** Each day AntWeb checks every specimen's name against AntCat. When AntCat has changed a name (a new combination or a genus synonymy) and the change is unambiguous, the specimen is filed and shown under the currently accepted name. The identification as uploaded is never overwritten: it stays on the specimen page as "Uploaded As", and previous determinations are unchanged. Each change is listed, with its reason and the AntCat reference, in the Name Update Report (`nameUpdates.do`).
- **Curators decide the ambiguous cases.** Specimens filed under a name AntCat still calls valid are not moved. When a genus was divided, specimens move only under a rule a curator has approved for a region; the rest are listed for the curator or the AntCat editors.
- **Upload completion email.** Curators get an email when a specimen upload finishes, with a summary and a link to the upload report.
- **Clearer upload reports.** Problems come first, then what was loaded. Non-ant records and ignored columns are listed once, and long specimen lists fold. Counts are given in specimens; only lines that were not loaded are counted as lines of your file.
- **Unidentified species written one way.** Forms such as "sp." and "*indet*" in the species or subspecies column are written as "(indet)" on upload, and the report says so.
- **Non-ant records.** A record filed under Formicidae with a subfamily or genus that is not an ant is refused, with the reason. Non-ants filed under their own family load as given.
- **Species List Tool.** When editing a long list, the subfamily choice offers only the ant subfamilies on that list.
- **Upload as.** The setting on your account page shows your current choices, so adding one keeps the others.
- **Welcome email.** New curator accounts receive an introductory email.

### API improvements
Two of these changes can affect existing scripts.
- **Full species names.** `/distinctTaxa?taxarank=species` returns each species once by its full name, for example *Pheidole megacephala*. Before, it returned only the species epithet, so species sharing an epithet in different genera were combined.
- **Exact country matching.** `country=` matches the whole country name, in upper or lower case, on `/specimens`, `/distinctTaxa`, `/measurements` and `/geolocaleTaxa`. For example, `country=Niger` no longer returns records from Nigeria. Scripts that relied on partial names should use the full country name.
- **Same counts as the site.** The counts returned by `/taxa` are copied each day from AntWeb's own counts, so the API and the website give the same numbers.

### Data quality and reliability
- **Written counting rules.** Every kind of number AntWeb shows follows a written rule, and automated checks after each daily update confirm that the counts stay consistent.
- **Testing before every change.** More than 1,600 automated end-to-end tests run on a staging copy of AntWeb before any change goes live, and every day. A release check confirms their results before each change is merged.
- **More reliable daily updates.** The daily update retries the AntCat download for up to an hour and reports any step that fails.
